# X-POS — Facebook & Instagram campaign

**Goal:** demo bookings via WhatsApp (Click-to-WhatsApp ads), plus landing-page visits.
**Offer in every ad:** a free demo on the shop owner's own kind of products.
**Creatives:** `ads/creatives/` — 1080×1080 PNGs, one per audience, in English and Sinhala. Source: `ads/creatives.html`.

> Fill `[WHATSAPP_NUMBER]` / `[LANDING_URL]` before publishing. Budget numbers below are starting suggestions — adjust after the first 7 days.

---

## Campaign structure

| Ad set | Audience | Language | Creative | Objective | Start budget / day |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Sri Lankan retail shops | SI + EN | `retail-*` | Engagement → WhatsApp messages | LKR 1,500 |
| B | Multi-branch businesses | EN + SI | `branches-*` | Engagement → WhatsApp messages | LKR 1,500 |
| C | Shops that want a web store | SI + EN | `online-*` | Traffic → landing page `#online-store` | LKR 1,500 |
| D | International SMEs | EN | `global-en` | Traffic → landing page | LKR 1,000 |
| R | Retargeting: site visitors + video viewers (30 days) | SI + EN | best performer | WhatsApp messages | LKR 700 |

Run A–D for 7 days, then move budget to the ad sets with the lowest cost per WhatsApp conversation.

### Targeting

**A — Retail shops (Sri Lanka, 25–60)**
- Interests: Small business, Retail, Shop owner, Entrepreneurship, Point of sale, Inventory
- Behaviours: Small business owners, Facebook page admins (business)
- Locations: all island; split test Western Province vs. rest of island

**B — Multi-branch businesses (Sri Lanka, 28–60)**
- Job titles: Owner, Director, Managing Director, General Manager, Operations Manager, Accountant
- Interests: Business management, Supply chain, Franchising, Accounting software
- Layer with: Small business owners

**C — Shops going online (Sri Lanka, 22–50)**
- Interests: E-commerce, Online shopping, Shopify, Daraz, PayHere, Facebook Marketplace, Digital marketing
- Behaviours: Facebook page admins (business), Small business owners

**D — International SMEs (English)**
- Countries to test: Maldives, UAE, Qatar, Oman, Kenya, Bangladesh
- Interests: Small business, Retail, Point of sale, Inventory management, Entrepreneurship
- Keep it small until a first overseas customer is signed

**Placements:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Facebook Marketplace, Stories (crop creative to 9:16 with the same layout).

## What to track

| Metric | Where | Target to aim for after week 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per WhatsApp conversation | Ads Manager | falling week on week |
| Conversations → demos booked | your WhatsApp / CRM | set your own baseline in week 1 |
| Demos → paying shops | your records | set your own baseline in week 1 |
| Landing page CTR (sets C, D) | Ads Manager | > 1% |

Keep a simple sheet: date, ad set, conversations, demos, sales. After 2 weeks, pause the weakest ad set and double the best one.
