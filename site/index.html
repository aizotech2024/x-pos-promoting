FAQ

Questions shop owners ask.

Do I need to buy a special computer?

No. X-POS runs in a web browser, so it works on the PC, laptop, tablet or phone you already have.

Which printers work with it?

80mm thermal receipt printers for bills, normal printers for A4 invoices, and barcode label printers for EAN-13 labels.

Can bills be in Sinhala?

Yes. X-POS generates PDF bills and invoices that print Sinhala text correctly.

Can staff see other branches?

Only if you allow it. Every user gets a role, and branch users can be limited to their own branch.

Is my data shared with other shops?

No. Each business runs on its own separate instance with its own database.

Can I move my existing products in?

Yes. Send us your product list and we'll import it during setup.
